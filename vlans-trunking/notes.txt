Simulated Scenario 
A small Nairobi office has 6 staff members.3 departments, Accounts, Sales and HR ,share a switch and are on the same subnet.
Each office has 2 PCs ,Acccounts PCs can see Sales and HR PCs can see the other to, broadcast traffic reach every device.Owner wants the 3 departments separated,while still letting them reach other through the router
##Problem##
Every department can see each other,broadcast traffic hit each other

##WHAT I LEARNT ## 
Do not configure native vlans on sub-interfaces meant for another vlan ,configure vlan on its own subinterface and make sure it matches with the native vlan on the sw
##R1 NATIVE VLAN Config##
int g0/0.99
en dot1q 99 native
ip add 192.168.99.1 255.255.255.0
##SW NATIVE VLAN##
int g0/1
sw trunk native vlan 99
