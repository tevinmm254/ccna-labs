Re-address of Nairobi Office
Ticket#002
Office is out of room on its flat 192.168.X.X plan and wastes addresses the owner wanted to assign addresses in accordance to the hosts needed.
One block was given to work with 192.168.50.0/24.It must cover

Segment               VLAN  Hosts needed
Sales                  10        50
Accounts               20        25
HR                     30        12
Servers                40        5
Link to ISP router (R2) n/a      2

There should also be room left for future expansion of 30 hosts.


###SOLUTION###
Assign each segment subnets in respect to the number of hosts needed:
Segment(Hosts)| VLAN| Network/Prefix   | Usable Range | Broadcast      | Gateway
Sales(50)       10     192.168.50.0/26    .1-.62        192.168.50.63   192.168.50.1
Accounts(25)    20     192.168.50.64/27   .65-.94       192.168.50.95   192.168.50.65
HR(12)          30     192.168.50.96/28   .97-.110      192.168.50.111  192.168.50.97
Servers(5)      40     192.168.50.112/29  .113-.118     192.168.50.119  192.168.50.113
Link to ISP(2)  n/a    192.168.50.120/30   .121-.122    192.168.50.123       -


Link to ISP Routers- R1-192.168.50.121 R2-192.168.50.122

   ##Future Expansion##
 2^n=number of hosts=2^6=64(64-2=62-> the number of usable add) .128 is a multiple of 64.
Cannot use /27 since we need 30 hosts since /27=30 hosts but the gateway is not part of the hosts so it gives 29 hosts.
 30hosts- 192.168.50.128/26-netwrok Add  192.168.50.129-1st usable add  192.168.50.190-Last usable 192.168.50.191-Broadcast Add.Gateway-192.168.50.129

    ##GAP##
The address 192.168.50.124 to 192.168.50.127 was left as a gap and it can be added to another subnet there are 4 addresses 124,125,126,127 .
2^2=4 32-2=30 prefix = /30 network=192.168.50.124/30 Range=.125-126 Broadcast=192.168.50.127

   ##LeftOver##
Addresses 192.168.50.192-192.168.50.255 were left I chose one subnet to put it all in
.192 to .255 = 64 add, 2^6 = 64 32-6=26 Prefix=/26
192.168.50.192/26-Network Range=.193-.254 Broadcast =192.168.50.255

###ERRORS ENCOUNTERED###
1.Used static route summary without the Null0 command at the end of the configuration
This led to a loop when an unused subnet in /24 is used eg 192.168.50.200 if pinged it will reach R1 since it unrecognized it will be treated as a default network which I configured to be sent to R2, R2 sees it part of /24 so it sends it back. The loops continue till TTL (time to live) is up.
Fix: On R1: Ip route 192.168.50.0 255.255.255.0 null0. It tells R1, "if any address from this subnet arrives and I don't know it drop"
##tracert loop##
##tracert fix##
###static summary route pic###

Lessons 
A subnet must start on a multiple of its block size, so you can’t always use the next free address.
A summary route needs a Null0 partner on the other router, or unused addresses loop.
VLANs must exist on every switch the trunk carries them to.
